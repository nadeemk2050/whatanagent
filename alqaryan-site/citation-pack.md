### 100 words — for standard directory profiles

Al Qaryan Industries LLC has traded and recycled plastics from Karachi since 2007.
We import virgin, off-grade and recycled polymers from Japan, the USA, Canada, Malaysia,
the UAE, Saudi Arabia and Indonesia, and process around 500 tons of material per month at
our own facility in the Shershah Industrial Area — shredding, washing, pelletising and two
PVC pipe extrusion lines. We buy scrap from factories, utilities, contractors and
collectors nationwide, and supply recycled HDPE, LDPE, PP, PET, PVC, engineering plastics,

### 250 words — for Alibaba, Europages, association profiles and your own "About" summary

Al Qaryan Industries LLC is a plastic trading and recycling house based in the Shershah
Industrial Area of Karachi, Pakistan, established in 2007.

We operate at the intersection of two markets. On the buying side, we purchase plastic
scrap from factories, utilities, municipal contractors, demolition firms and collectors
across Pakistan, and we import baled and shredded feedstock from the UAE, Saudi Arabia,
Malaysia, Indonesia, Japan, the USA and Canada. On the selling side, we supply
manufacturers with recycled and virgin polymers in the form they need — regrind, washed
flake, agglomerate, pellets, bales and purges — together with off-grade material for
cost-sensitive applications and prime resin with full producer documentation.

Our facility processes around 500 tons of plastic per month. It runs a shredding line, a
washing and drying line, a pelletiser with filtration, and two PVC pipe extrusion lines,
which means we consume a proportion of our own recycled compound internally and can
therefore keep buying scrap when the external compound market is quiet.

The materials we handle include HDPE (including PE100, PE80 and PE63 pipe scrap), LDPE,
LLDPE, PP, PET, PVC — both rigid and flexible — polycarbonate and other engineering
plastics. Our PVC pipe range serves drainage, sewer, conduit and irrigation applications.

We work to ISO 9001 and ISO 14001, hold REACH and SGS documentation, and issue a batch
test report with each lot. Where a specification depends on the lot — melt flow, intrinsic
viscosity, filler content — we measure it and report it rather than quoting a figure we
cannot hold to. Deliveries move by road nationwide, with container lots available for
export enquiries.

## 4. Directory checklist — worth the effort vs not

### Tier 1 — do these first, they carry real weight

| Where | Why it matters | Cost | Who does it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Google Business Profile** | Feeds Google's knowledge graph and the local pack for "plastic recycling Karachi". The single highest-value listing that exists. | Free | You (needs phone/postcard verification) |
| **Bing Places for Business** | Syncs to Bing, which feeds Copilot and part of ChatGPT search | Free, imports from GBP | You (2 min, sign in with Microsoft) |
| **LinkedIn Company Page** | Strong entity signal, and a `sameAs` target for your Organization schema | Free | You |
| **Pakistan Plastic Manufacturers Association (PPMA)** | The one association that actually matters in this industry in Pakistan; gives a genuine industry citation | Paid membership | You (requires application) |

### Tier 2 — worthwhile, moderate effort

| Where | Notes |
|---|---|
| **FPCCI** (Federation of Pakistan Chambers of Commerce & Industry) | Respectable, membership-based. Good for tender credibility. |
| **Sindh / Karachi Chamber of Commerce** | Same, regional. Useful if you tender for municipal work. |
| **Alibaba supplier profile** | Real export enquiry flow. Requires verification and often a paid tier to rank. |
| **Europages / Kompass** | Free basic listings, decent for European buyer discovery. |
| **Made-in-Pakistan style B2B portals** | Varies. Take the free tier only. |

### Tier 3 — skip, or take only the free listing

- Generic "business directory Pakistan" sites that email you weekly about upgrading
- Any directory that asks for payment before showing you where the listing appears
- "Submit your site to 500 directories" services — these produce spam links that Google
  discounts and that can actively harm trust
- Link exchanges and "SEO packages" promising 1000 backlinks

**How to spot a link farm:** if the directory lists plumbers, wedding photographers and
plastic recyclers on the same page, with no editorial control and an "upgrade to premium"
button on every row, it is worthless. If you cannot find a real company you recognise
listed alongside yours, walk away.

## 6. Honest expectation setting
Citations do not produce rankings overnight. What they do is make it unambiguous, to both
Google and to AI models, that Al Qaryan Industries LLC is a real, operating, locatable
business in a specific industry at a specific address. That is the foundation everything
else sits on — and right now the business exists on exactly one domain, which is thin.

Realistically: Google Business Profile will start influencing local queries within days of
verification. Directory citations take a few weeks to be crawled and acknowledged. AI
citation in ChatGPT or Perplexity is not something you can measure directly — but it
follows from being indexed, being corroborated, and being the clearest source for a
specific fact.
