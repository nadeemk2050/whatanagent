Fill in the bracketed fields from your company documents before sending.
**Never** guess a registration number.

### Documents you'll need (all three bodies ask for these)
- [ ] SECP incorporation certificate
- [ ] NTN certificate (FBR)
- [ ] Sales tax registration (if applicable)
- [ ] CNIC of the authorised signatory
- [ ] Company letterhead + stamp
- [ ] Latest bank account maintenance certificate (FPCCI/KCCI)
- [ ] Factory photos + plant list (PPMA likes this)

### 2a. Short description — 100 words (use on every form)
```
Al Qaryan Industries (Pvt) Ltd is a plastic recycling, polymer trading and PVC pipe
manufacturing company based in Shershah Industrial Area, Karachi, established in 2007.
We buy plastic scrap from factories, utilities and collectors across Pakistan and import
baled and shredded feedstock from the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Malaysia, Indonesia, Japan, the USA
and Canada. We supply manufacturers with recycled HDPE, LDPE, LLDPE, PP, PET, PVC and
engineering polymers as regrind, washed flake, agglomerate, pellets and bales, plus off-grade

washing line, pelletiser and two PVC pipe extrusion lines. ISO 9001 and ISO 14001.
```

### 2b. Long description — 250 words (PPMA / KCCI membership profile)
```
Al Qaryan Industries (Pvt) Ltd has operated in Pakistan's plastics sector since 2007, from a
facility in the Shershah Industrial Area of Karachi. The company works at both ends of the
plastic chain: it buys scrap and industrial waste from factories, utilities, municipal
contractors, demolition firms and independent collectors across Pakistan, and imports baled,
shredded and off-grade feedstock from the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Malaysia, Indonesia, Japan, the
USA and Canada.

On the supply side, Al Qaryan Industries delivers recycled and virgin polymers to manufacturers
as regrind, washed flake, agglomerate, pellets, bales and purges, together with off-grade
material for cost-sensitive applications and prime resin with full producer documentation.
Grades handled include HDPE (PE100, PE80 and PE63 pipe scrap), LDPE, LLDPE, PP, PET, PVC in
rigid and flexible form, polycarbonate and other engineering plastics.

The Karachi plant processes approximately 500 tons of plastic per month using a shredding line,
a washing and drying line, a pelletiser with filtration, and two PVC pipe extrusion lines which
also consume a share of the company's own recycled compound. The PVC pipe range covers
drainage, sewer, conduit and irrigation applications.

The company operates to ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 and holds REACH and SGS documentation.
Deliveries move by road nationwide, with container lots available for export enquiries.
Al Qaryan Industries employs more than fifty people and is registered as a private limited
company in Pakistan.

Website: https://alqaryangroup.com
Contact: +92 333 201 0506 · info@alqaryangroup.com
```


### 2d. Where to apply
| Body | What it is | How |
|---|---|---|
| **PPMA** — Pakistan Plastic Manufacturers Association | The trade body that matters for plastics in Pakistan | Application form via PPMA site; needs plant details + photos. Ask for the current fee schedule — do not send payment before receiving an invoice. |
| **FPCCI** — Federation of Pakistan Chambers of Commerce & Industry | Federal chamber; strong entity citation | Apply through your local chamber first; FPCCI membership usually follows. |
| **KCCI** — Karachi Chamber of Commerce & Industry | Local chamber for Karachi (base city) | Online membership form; attach SECP + NTN + bank certificate. |
