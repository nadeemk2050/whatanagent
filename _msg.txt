Report when the stored Drive token was issued

/gdrive/diagnose now returns tokenStoredAt (mtime of the token file). Combined
with the moment the OAuth app was published, that answers the only question that
matters: was this token minted while the app was still in Testing (so Google
expires it ~7 days later), or after publishing (permanent)? Without it the
answer was guesswork.
