What we trade

Metal scrap and recyclable raw material, bought and sold by grade — not by guesswork.

Ferrous metal scrap

  • Heavy melting scrap (HMS 1 & 2)
  • Shredded and plate & structural scrap
  • Cast iron, rails, rebar offcuts

Non-ferrous metal scrap

  • Copper: millberry, birch/cliff, cable
  • Aluminium: extrusion, taint, dross
  • Brass, bronze and mixed metals

Recycled polymers

  • PET bottles, flakes and regrind
  • HDPE jerry cans, drums, crates
  • PP / PPCP, PC, PVC, jumbo & rafia bags

Industrial by-products

  • Aluminium dross and skimmings
  • Processing offcuts and production waste
  • Mixed loads sorted on request
