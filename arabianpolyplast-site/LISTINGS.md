## House rules (these matter more than the copy)

1. **Same NAP everywhere** — a different phone number on one listing weakens all of them.
2. **One listing per material**, not one for the company.
3. **Reply within 24 hours.** Directory enquiries are usually buyers comparing three suppliers.
4. **Refresh monthly.** Ageing listings sink; editing bumps them.
5. **Link back** to `https://arabianpolyplast.com` wherever a website field exists.
6. **Send me the profile URLs** when they exist. I'll add them to the site's `sameAs` schema, which is the strongest signal that all these profiles are the same company.
