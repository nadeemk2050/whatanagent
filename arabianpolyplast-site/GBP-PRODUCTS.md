## Two rules before you start

1. **Leave the price empty.** The site deliberately quotes per market day; a fixed
   number in the profile contradicts it and goes stale in a week. If Google insists
   on a number, put a round *indicative* figure and update it every month — but the
   description already says "quote per load", which is the honest answer.
2. **Capitalise properly.** `pc bottle scrap in oman` shows exactly as typed. Use
   `PC bottle scrap in Oman`. No ALL CAPS, no keyword stuffing — the product name is
   visible to buyers and one unnatural name cheapens the whole profile.
